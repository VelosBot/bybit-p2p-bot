## Why a Bybit P2P bot?

Bybit P2P is competitive. The merchants priced one tick better than everyone else take the volume. Manually keeping multiple ads at the right price 24/7 across multiple coins is impossible without automation. A **Bybit P2P bot** automates this:

- Monitors every competitor ad on the Bybit P2P market across USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH
- Re-prices your ads to stay competitive
- Filters out small / dust ads that aren't real competition
- Falls back to a safe price when no real competitors exist
- Runs 24/7 so you don't have to

## Installation

1. **Get a 7-day free trial.** Visit [velosbot.com/bots/bybit#pricing](https://velosbot.com/bots/bybit#pricing) and request a trial license — no payment, no card.
2. **Download** `velosbot.exe` (Windows desktop client) and the **VelosBot Android APK** — links sent with your trial license.
3. **Create a Bybit API key.** In Bybit → API Management → Create API → System-generated → API Permissions = **Read-Write** → scroll to Fiat Trading → tick only **Ads** (leave everything else unchecked). Save. We recommend whitelisting your home IP if you have a static one.
4. **Configure the bot.** Paste your license key, Bybit API key, and secret into velosbot.exe. Open the Android app, enter the same license key, and configure your coins and ads.
5. **Click Start.** The bot connects to our cloud, reads your settings, and starts updating your Bybit P2P ads automatically.

## FAQ

**Is my Bybit API key safe?**
Yes. Your key stays on your PC, stored locally. **Our server never sees your API key** — even if we got hacked, your funds are safe. The "Fiat Trading → Ads only" permission can update your P2P ad prices and nothing else — it physically cannot withdraw, transfer, or trade. We also recommend whitelisting your home IP if you have a static one.

**Which Bybit P2P coins are supported?**
USDT, USDC, BTC, and ETH — all four with full feature parity.

**How many ads can I run?**
Up to **2 Buy + 2 Sell ads per coin** — that's 16 ads total when you run all 4 coins. Each ad has its own pricing rules, limits, and target competitors.

**How fast is the auto-pricing?**
The bot updates your ads **every minute** based on the latest Bybit P2P market state.
